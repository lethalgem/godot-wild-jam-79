# PolyDash

### [Game Link](https://lethalgem.itch.io/polydash)

For submission to [Godot Wild Jam #79](https://itch.io/jam/godot-wild-jam-79)

## Genre

Platformer

## Tagline

"Dash, jump, and survive the rhythm of growing platforms —where precision and speed are your only allies!"

## Description

Welcome to PolyDash, a fast-paced, adrenaline-pumping 3D platformer where quick reactions and precise timing are key! Dash through a series of poly-shaped obstacles in a vibrant, dynamic world that reacts to the beat of a high-energy soundtrack. Each section offers a different set of challenges, with platforms that grow and shrink to the rhythm of the music, demanding perfect timing to survive.

## Controls

- W, A, S, D: Move your character in 3D space.
- Space: Jump.
- Shift: Dash for a burst of speed.
- Double Space: Perform a double jump.
- Escape: Open the in-game menu.

## Key Features

- Unique Obstacles: Navigate through various poly-shaped obstacles and sections that keep the gameplay fresh and exciting.
- Beat-Synchronized Platforms: Platforms grow and shrink based on the tempo of the music, creating an additional layer of challenge.
- Adrenaline-Fueled Gameplay: Quick restarts, fast-paced action, and the thrill of racing through difficult paths.
- Game Over Twist: At the end of your run, your fall/death counter is hilariously displayed as "bodies" on the Game Over screen, adding a touch of dark humor.
- High-Energy Soundtrack: A high BPM track pushes you forward as you race through each level, syncing perfectly with the game's rhythm.
- Single-Player: Dive in for a solo challenge that’s both difficult and satisfying!

## Why Play PolyDash?

With its challenging gameplay mechanics, fun beat-syncing obstacles, and fast restarts, PolyDash offers a quick and addictive experience for those looking to push their platforming skills to the limit. The timing-based difficulty and the playful endgame twist will keep you returning for more!

But don’t just focus on the challenge—have fun with it! **How many times did you fall?** Comment below with your death count! It's all part of the fun in PolyDash—each fall brings you closer to mastering the game (and providing us with some laughs). Let’s see who can get the lowest death count or who can embrace the chaos the most!
